# Banished — Worldbook

> The lore canon for *Banished*. Companion to DESIGN.md (systems) and
> GRIMOIRE.md (magic). Working names are marked ⚑ — easy to change later.

---

## The world you lost: the Ordinate ⚑

A vast continental empire — **not cruel, but absolute.** It rules by law and
ledger: every road patrolled, every trade taxed and recorded, every life
accounted for. Crime is low. Prosperity is high. Streets are clean and safe.
And it is **suffocating** — a place where stepping out of line is unthinkable
because the line is everywhere. The Ordinate believes, sincerely, that it is
doing right. That's what makes it work, and what makes it grim.

## The Phylactery & banishment (why you can't go home)

At majority, every citizen's soul is anchored to a **phylactery** — a warded
vessel kept in the **Spire of Wardens** on the continent. To be *banished* is
to have your phylactery **sequestered**: your name struck from the living rolls.
The Spire's wards know every sequestered soul; should one set foot on the
continent again, it is **smitten** on sight. Exile is therefore metaphysical,
not merely a long boat ride. You cannot sneak back. You are, by writ of soul,
gone.

- **Only soft crimes earn the boat.** Sedition, smuggling, debt, unsanctioned
  magic, heresy of order. **Blood-crimes — murder, rape — earn the headsman**,
  not banishment. (This is why the player picks a *survivable* crime.)
- **Passage:** the **prison-barge** *Verdict* ⚑ — a slab-sided state warship that
  crosses the treacherous Sunder Strait, unloads its manifest, and never carries
  a soul back. The only lawful way on or off, and it goes one direction that
  matters.
- **Soul-essence (vis):** the island is steeped in unanchored soul-stuff. Mages
  harvest it as **vis** from the dying — abundant here *because* this is where
  the soul-sundered are sent. (Lore reason our magic/enchant economy exists.)

## The island: Ashfall, called "the Pit"

Big, varied, and **ungoverned by the Ordinate** — the Empire only delivers, it
does not rule here. In the vacuum, exiles built their own rough order. Factions
hold the regions; power is real and uneven.

### Regions & factions (canon geography)

- **The Landing — Saltmarsh Town** *(start; built)*. Where the *Verdict* unloads.
  Shacks and a few modest houses; moderately prosperous but unremarkable — home
  to the meek and the unambitious who never made it inland. A weary
  **Warden-Scribe** records each newcomer and points them onward.
- **The Capital — Cinderhold ⚑ (centre).** Palace, fat mansions, and enormous
  slums pressed against the walls. The nearest thing to government, run on the
  Ordinate's inherited habits. *Guilds:* the **Magistracy** (order, bounties,
  clerking) and the **slum syndicate** (theft, smuggling).
- **The South — the Tilled Reaches.** Civilised exiles who keep the basic rules
  of law and want a quiet life. *Guild:* a **merchants' & wardens' concord**.
- **The West — the Unbound.** No law at all; raider holds and duelists. "Go there
  promptly," says the Warden, "if rules offend you." *Guild:* the **Free
  Companies**.
- **The East — the Pyramid.** Dark mages and necromancers raising a black
  ziggurat from the ash. Where the "suspicious mages" the Ordinate feared end up.
  *Guild:* the **Ashen Circle** (Umbramancy, forbidden research).

## The Warden's welcome (intro scene, deadpan)

> "Name's recorded. Soul's in the Spire. You'll not see the continent again —
> spare us both the speech about how it's a mistake.
> South, if you mean to keep the law: the Reaches will have you.
> West, if you don't: the Unbound ask no questions and offer no mercy. Go
> promptly, please.
> East, the Pyramid — I'm obliged to note it's discouraged.
> The Capital sits at the heart, should you fancy crowds and cutpurses.
> Welcome to Ashfall. Try to last the season."

## Character creation: choose your crime

Our take on Morrowind's class/birthsign — **you pick the crime that got you
banished**, and it shapes your build and how factions first see you.

| Crime | Bias | First leaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Sedition** (spoke against the Ordinate) | Personality, Speechcraft, Astromancy | dissidents, mages |
| **Smuggling** (moved contraband) | Speed/Agility, Athletics, Blade | the Unbound |
| **Forbidden Magic** (proscribed arts) | Willpower, Elementalism, Veilcraft | the Pyramid |
| **Ruinous Debt** (owed a life's worth) | Luck, Personality; extra coin/vis | the law-abiding |

## Design principle: a guild per city (Morrowind-style)

Each major city hosts a **guild** with its own questline, flavour, ranks, and
rewards — and they can conflict. Joining the Magistracy sours the syndicate;
the Pyramid and the Reaches despise each other. Reputation (built on the
disposition system we already have) is the connective tissue.
